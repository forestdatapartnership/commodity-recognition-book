Bushland and Grassland

Overview

Bushland and grassland with scattered shrubs can be challenging to differentiate from coffee or cocoa systems, particularly in imagery with resolutions above one meter, where individual trees are not clearly visible. However, a key distinguishing feature is the lack of structured canopy organization typically associated with cultivated systems like coffee and cocoa. In high-resolution imagery, the natural, irregular arrangement of bushes and shrubs in bushland and grassland becomes apparent, contrasting with the clear planting patterns seen in agricultural systems.
