Coffee Recognition

Coffee cultivation is a diverse and intricate agricultural practice, shaped by environmental conditions, cultural traditions, and economic considerations. From traditional systems that blend seamlessly into natural landscapes to modern, highly organized plantations, coffee farming varies significantly across regions. High-resolution imagery provides a valuable tool for analyzing these systems, offering insights into their structure, management, and unique characteristics. This guide explores the key aspects of coffee cultivation and how they appear in high-resolution imagery.
