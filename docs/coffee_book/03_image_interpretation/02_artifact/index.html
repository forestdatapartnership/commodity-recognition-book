Terrain Artifacts

Overview

Coffee is predominantly cultivated in mountainous environments, where slopes create unique visual challenges in high-resolution imagery. The same coffee system can appear strikingly different depending on the slope effect and the angle of the shadows cast by the terrain. These slope artifacts can distort the perception of planting patterns, tree density, or canopy structure, making it difficult to accurately interpret or classify coffee systems.
