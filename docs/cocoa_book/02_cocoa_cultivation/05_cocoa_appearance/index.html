Cocoa Tree Characteristics

Cocoa trees are medium-sized, with mature heights typically ranging from four to eight meters, though some trees may grow as tall as twelve meters in favorable conditions. The tree has large, leathery leaves that are arranged alternately in a flat, two-row (distichous) pattern along the branches. The leaves are oval to elliptical in shape, measuring between 12 and 60 cm in length and between 4 and 20 cm in width. Leaf tips are pointed, and the bases are usually rounded or heart-shaped.

One of the most distinctive visual features is the color of young leaves, or flushes, which emerge reddish-pink before gradually turning light green as they mature.
