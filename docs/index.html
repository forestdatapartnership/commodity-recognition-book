Commodity Recognition Book

Introduction

The use of satellite imagery to identify commodities is critical for understanding the impact of agriculture on natural systems, and for the successful implementation of conservation policies like the European Regulation on Deforestation-Free Products (EUDR). The scale of global agriculture demands that this identification be supported with artificial intelligence (AI); however, in many production systems, commodities are difficult to disambiguate from each other and from natural forests, so accurate identification still requires specialized human expertise. This limits the community’s ability to train and evaluate AI models, and in fact limits the community’s ability to even agree on evaluation procedures for AI systems.

This guide book attempts to democratize that expertise by providing tools and examples that will help readers recognize coffee and cocoa in satellite images in a variety of production systems.
